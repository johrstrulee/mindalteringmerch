Headgear is an attempt at turning an obsession with arts and crafts into a little business. Altough, getting my work "out there" for others to enjoy or hate (and hopefully having some interesting conversations, interactions, collaborations, and arguments because of it) is my primary motivation, being able to eat and pay my rent would be pretty nice as well.

Headgear makes things you might find in a headshop. Paraphernelia for you paraphernelia. We are just getting started so for now it's just prints, t-shirts, posters, stickers, schwagg, and one of a kind hand made toys for grown ups (but not adults). Bendable, posable, rubbery characters, creatures, and critters with ridiculously long limbs and big hands that can wrap around and grab and hold and dangle. As we evolve our product line will grow to include myriad smoking accessories, devices that hide, lock, and stash, a full clothing line, various gadgets, widgets, and devices, hardware and software skins, and exoskeletons, and so many strange and wonderful hand sculpted, carved, assembled, and fabricated stone, wood, metal, silicone, plastic, glass, characters, critters, and creatures for your garden, books shelf, table, or toy box.

We happily take requests, custom orders, and commisions. If you have something specific in mind we would love to hear about it and if we do not know how to make it already we can figure it out or find someone that does to give us a hand.
